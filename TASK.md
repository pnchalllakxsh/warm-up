# PromptWars Challenge: A Cooking To-Do List

## Challenge Overview
Build a simple AI micro-app that helps a user generate a personal cooking to-do list based on their day.

## Key Requirements
The micro-app should include a structured meal planning flow that produces:
- **Breakfast/Lunch/Dinner plan**: A complete daily meal plan.
- **Grocery list**: A compiled list of ingredients needed for the generated plan.
- **Substitutions**: Alternative ingredient suggestions (e.g., for dietary restrictions or unavailability).
- **Budget feasibility logic**: A mechanism to estimate or check the budget feasibility of the meal plan.
